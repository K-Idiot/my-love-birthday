A birthday little journey

For My Love

Before you open this, I want you to know one thing: I made this because I want that beautiful smile of yours to stay around for a very, very long time.

Do you remember how it started?

There was this completely ordinary Call of Duty: Mobile match. Five people, a random team, nothing that looked like the beginning of anything.

Then I suddenly heard a soft, cute voice telling someone where the enemy was. My mic was open, I was playing with my brother, and I immediately went:

“Bhaiya, bhaiya, apne suna!? Yeh kisi ladki ki awaaz hai… koi ladki hai.” 😂

The funniest part? I didn't even know who the voice belonged to. Someone else started claiming it was his voice and began playing mind games with us.

Eventually I figured out that it was you — hidden behind streamer mode. I couldn't add you. So I waited… and waited… and, yes, I stalked your game ID. 😂

You had seen my game ID too, apparently, and after weeks I finally managed to add you. And that's when a random match stopped being random.

somewhere along the way
I didn't just start loving your voice.
I started loving my days with you.

Then came us.

We talked all night. We jammed to songs. We downloaded Discord just to watch movies together. We stayed on calls until sleep won.

And somewhere between all those little things, you became part of my everyday life.

I think I fell for you the first time I heard your voice. I knew I loved you when your absence started feeling like an emptiness.

We've had so many memories, even with all these miles between us. And somehow, you've still managed to make me feel incredibly close to you.

A little collection of my cutuu 💛

You say it's just another picture. I see another reason to smile. I could have filled this entire website with your photos and still have more to add.

There is something I need you to know.

I don't have five things I love about you. I have way too many, and I don't think I could ever finish listing them.

I love your angry face. I love you when you're upset. I love your silly faces, your cute nose, your lips, your curly hair, your eyelashes and those beautiful eyes.

I love the parts of you that you might not always love yourself. Your flaws don't make me love you less. They're part of the person I fell in love with.

But more than all of that, I love your soul. I love the person you are.

And more than anything, my love, I want you to be happy. I want you to smile — that real, uncontrollable smile — not just today, but on ordinary Tuesdays, terrible days, sleepy mornings, and every random little moment in between.

If I can be one of the reasons you smile a little more, I'll consider myself incredibly lucky.
